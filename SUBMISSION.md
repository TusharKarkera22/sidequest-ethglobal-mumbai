# Sidequest

**Tagline:** Find your missing piece. A wallet-owned, expiring task board for builders.

Sidequest helps teams find someone who can unblock their next step. Publish a focused ask, discover requests by skill, claim work and close the loop. Arkiv stores public, wallet-owned task and action records whose lifetime matches the request.

## What is included

A responsive frontend, Worker backend, D1 rehearsal workspace and official Arkiv SDK integration. Search, categories, ownership, expiry, status, wallet setup and optional bounty offers are implemented. No payments or escrow are processed.

## Sponsor target

Arkiv — Build on Arkiv (from scratch). A second app can query the same schema without a Sidequest account. Separate action entities allow builders to coordinate without editing the publisher's record.

## Submission evidence

This public edition uses a fresh namespace and excludes historical wallet-linked evidence for privacy. Generate current evidence with your own test wallet and provide what the prize track requires. Do not describe the practice workspace as onchain data.
