# Who Sidequest is for

Intended users are hackathon builders needing a short unblock, mentors browsing concrete asks, and agents capable of discovering small tasks through a public data protocol.

The demo can be tried through Practice without wallet setup. The live board can be read without connecting a wallet. Publishing needs a Rabdi wallet funded with test tGLM.

No adoption counts, interviews, retention or willingness-to-pay results are claimed. The initial board has eight labelled practice examples, not real customers or open bounties.

After Mumbai, proposed validation is to run a board for one event, measure time to claim and completed tasks, and interview both requesters and helpers. These are plans, not completed research. Notification and agent-client support are proposed follow-ups.
